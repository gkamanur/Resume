## 💡 Interview Preparation

### Demonstrate Your SOC Lab

**Prepare a 10-15 Minute Demo:**
1. **Architecture Overview** (2 min)
   - Show docker-compose.yml
   - Explain container networking
   - Highlight ELK + Wazuh + Suricata stack

2. **Detection Demonstration** (5 min)
   - Run attack scenario (e.g., brute force)
   - Show logs in Kibana
   - Demonstrate alert firing
   - Show detection rule (KQL)

3. **Investigation Walkthrough** (5 min)
   - Triage alert
   - Correlate events across sources
   - Explain MITRE ATT&CK mapping
   - Discuss containment strategy

4. **Q&A** (3 min)
   - Explain technical choices
   - Discuss scalability
   - Show willingness to learn

### Common SOC Interview Questions

**Technical:**
- "Walk me through how you'd investigate a suspicious login alert"
- "What's the difference between IDS and IPS?"
- "Explain the MITRE ATT&CK framework"
- "How would you detect lateral movement in a network?"
- "What logs would you prioritize for security monitoring?"

**Behavioral:**
- "Why transition from development to cybersecurity?"
  → *Answer: Production support exposed me to security incidents, sparked interest*
- "How do you stay current with evolving threats?"
  → *Answer: Labs, certifications, security blogs, CTFs*
- "Describe a time you solved a complex problem under pressure"
  → *Answer: Use production support examples*

**Scenario-Based:**
- "You receive 500 alerts. How do you prioritize?"
  → *Answer: Risk-based triage, severity + asset criticality*
- "Multiple users report slow network performance. What do you check?"
  → *Answer: Check for DDoS, malware communication, data exfiltration*

### German Interview Tips
- **Punctuality**: Arrive 10 min early (or join video call 5 min early)
- **Formal address**: Use "Sie" (formal you) unless told otherwise
- **Dress code**: Business casual minimum, business formal for financial sector
- **Handshake**: Firm, brief, with eye contact
- **Questions**: Prepare 2-3 thoughtful questions about the role
- **Language**: Most tech interviews are in English, but show German effort
